## What to watch

- **Water.** The San Patricio Municipal Water District buys its water from Corpus Christi and resells it to Odem, Taft, Gregory, Portland, Ingleside, Ingleside-on-the-Bay, Aransas Pass and others. It also supplies many of the region's large industrial water users. The ExxonMobil-SABIC plastics plant in the county, Gulf Coast Growth Ventures, is the largest water consumer in the Corpus Christi region. A very rainy July pulled the region back from a water emergency that was expected this year. Corpus Christi's models have narrowed the projected date of a Level 1 Water Emergency to September 2029.
- **Republican primaries.** All five seats on the Commissioners Court are held by Republicans. County Judge David Krebs kept the Republican nomination by 47 votes in March 2026, 2,890 to 2,843 over Mark Roach. Sheriff Oscar Rivera won his 2024 Republican primary over Daniel Lorberau by 74 votes. The sheriff's office and the Precinct 1 and 3 commissioner seats are up in 2028.
- **2028 turnout.** The Democratic gains came in a midterm with [[turnout.2026.ballots|count]] ballots cast, compared with 25,734 in 2024. The test is whether the margin holds when turnout returns to presidential levels, measured against the 2024 result of [[marginHistory.2024.margin|margin]].

San Patricio's margin in the top races shrank in 2026, while its local offices moved further Republican.
