## What to watch

- **Water.** A Texas Water Development Board evaluation in 1989 found that almost all of the county's water comes from the High Plains aquifer system, the Ogallala. In February 2026, Groundwater Management Area 1, the regional planning group, proposed keeping at least 40% of the Ogallala's stored volume in Dallam, Hartley, Moore and Sherman counties through 2080. One state monitoring well in the county measured 434.56 feet below the surface on Oct. 23, 2026, 40.84 feet deeper than in September 2013. A bi-county commissioners court meeting posted by Dallam County Judge Wes Ritchey for Aug. 24, 2026 included an item on subdivision water concerns.
- **Republican primaries.** With no Democrats running for county office in 2026, the Republican primary in March settled every county race. Commissioner Precincts 1 and 3 are next on the ballot in 2028.
- **2028 turnout.** The 2026 Senate margin came with [[turnout.2026.ballots|count]] ballots cast, compared with 1,442 votes for president in 2024, when Donald Trump carried the county by [[marginHistory.2024.margin|margin]]. The 2028 presidential race will show how much of the 2026 drop holds.

Dallam stayed solidly Republican in the 2026 Senate race, with a margin about 34 points smaller than in the 2022 governor's race.
