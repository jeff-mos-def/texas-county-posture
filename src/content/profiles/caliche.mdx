## What to watch

- **May 2027, Caliche Springs ISD bond.** The first local race since the shift. Watch Northgate turnout without a statewide race on the ballot.
- **Commissioners court.** Whether the Precinct 1 flip changes votes on Northgate roads and the budget.
- **November 2028.** The first presidential test against the 2024 baseline of [[marginHistory.2024.margin|margin]].

Caliche County is still Republican. The next election will show whether 2026 was about the candidates or the county.
