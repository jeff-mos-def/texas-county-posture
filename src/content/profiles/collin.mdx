Republican Duncan Webb won that seat in 2022 with 53.19%. In the county's official count, Democrat Angie Carraway unseated Republican Rep. Candy Noble in House District 89, 50.83% to 49.17%.

## What to watch

- **The bond do-over.** Voters rejected all four county bond propositions, $817.5 million in all, for roads, court and detention facilities, parks and an elections and records storage facility. The $475 million roads proposition came closest, failing with 51.63% against, while the elections facility drew 69.90% against. County officials are weighing a new plan for 2027.
- **Close races.** In the county's official count, Republican Rep. Keresa Richardson led Democrat Brittany Black by 7 votes in House District 61, and Republican Rep. Jeff Leach led Democrat Jordan Wheatley by 128 in House District 67. From January, Holmer will be the only Democrat on the five-member Commissioners Court. Precinct 1 Commissioner Susan Fletcher, unopposed in 2024, and Precinct 3 Commissioner Darrell Hale, who won with 61.34% in 2024, are on the 2028 ballot.
- **2028 turnout.** The 2026 Senate margin came with [[turnout.2026.ballots|count]] ballots cast, compared with 520,196 in 2024, when Donald Trump carried the county by [[marginHistory.2024.margin|margin]]. The 2028 presidential race will show how much of that reversal holds.

Collin moved from a Republican margin in the 2022 governor's race to a Democratic margin in the 2026 Senate race, while Republicans held the county judge and county clerk offices, each by less than 2.5 points.
